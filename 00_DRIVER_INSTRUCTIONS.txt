DRIVER INSTRUCTIONS - LIFT AND CLAW ROBOT
========================================
Current controls and settings. Angles below are MOTOR encoder degrees,
not the physical lift angle after external gearing.

QUICK CONTROLS
--------------
Left stick up/down : Drive forward/backward.
Right stick left/right : Steer.
L1 - HOLD : Raise the lift. Release to hold its position.
L2 - HOLD : Lower manually at 40 RPM. Release to hold its position.
D-pad Down - HOLD : Lower slowly for scoring at 60 RPM. Release to open the
                    claw and start the scoring wait/automatic return below.
R1 - HOLD : Run the intake inward only. Release to stop.
R2 - HOLD : Run the intake outward only. Release to stop.
D-pad Right - PRESS : Reset the lift to encoder zero.
Y - PRESS : Toggle the main claw open/closed.
Reset continues after releasing Right and does NOT recalibrate encoder zero.
Claw tilt is fixed EXTENDED on ADI G; no button or macro changes it.

BEFORE STARTING THE PROGRAM
---------------------------
1. Place the lift at its physical bottom before starting/restarting the program.
   The program records this starting position as 0 degrees; it does not home
   the lift automatically. Do not restart with the lift raised.
2. Keep the robot still while the IMU calibrates, roughly three seconds.
3. Startup closes the claw for the preload and records it as CLOSED.
   The first Y click therefore OPENS the claw.
   The separate claw tilt cylinder stays EXTENDED in its normal position.
4. When driver control starts, the closed preload is raised to the 10-degree
   clearance minimum. Opening the claw near the floor allows a return to zero.

LIFT MINIMUM AND GROUND CLEARANCE
--------------------------------
Manual lowering, claw CLOSED : Minimum is 10 degrees above encoder zero.
Manual lowering, claw OPEN   : Minimum is 0 degrees.
Button/macro reset           : Returns to 0 degrees regardless of claw state.
Upper limit                 : Currently 300 degrees (a placeholder to tune).

Manual downward movements respect the current manual minimum.
A completed reset holds zero, even with the claw closed. A new close-claw
command below 10 degrees raises the lift to 10 degrees and leaves reset mode.
Closing it above 10 degrees holds the current height; it does not pull the
lift down or add another 10 degrees. Repeated toggles do not stack height.

Opening manually near the floor returns the lift to zero only when BOTH
motor encoders are within 20 degrees of zero. Opening high up does not
automatically drop the lift.

SCORING WITH D-PAD DOWN
----------------------
1. Use L1 to raise the lift to the height you need, then release L1.
   The lift uses the built-in PROS HOLD brake.
2. Hold D-pad Down to lower slowly toward the score. Release when positioned.
3. Releasing D-pad Down opens the claw ONCE and holds the lift where it stopped.
   Pressing Y in that same control-loop iteration takes priority over the
   automatic opening; the scoring wait still starts normally.
4. Wait at least 250 milliseconds for the claw, then drive away at least
   3 inches (about 7.6 cm) from where D-pad Down was released. Moving away during
   that brief delay also counts, but lowering waits for the delay to finish.
5. The lift automatically resets to 0 degrees at normal speed and holds there.

Pull-away detection requires throttle input AND measured translation.
Turning in place alone does not count. Pushing the stick while the robot
is blocked does not count unless odometry reports the required distance.
If no pull-away is detected, the lift keeps holding.

You may toggle Y during the scoring wait. It does not cancel or restart
the wait and does not move a lift held above the clearance minimum.
Reclosing below the minimum raises only to the clearance height.

RESET AND INTERRUPTING A SEQUENCE
--------------------------------
D-pad Right cancels the pending lift sequence and returns directly to encoder
zero, without opening or closing the claw. Reset continues after release.
Repeated reset presses only request zero again; there is no tilt action.

Holding Right gives reset priority over the other lift controls. After releasing
Right, holding L1, L2, or D-pad Down interrupts the reset. These controls also
interrupt the scoring wait/automatic return. L1 wins over either down control;
L2 wins over D-pad Down.
Disabling the robot cancels pending lift sequences.

BRAIN DISPLAY
--------------
The Brain shows "Lift: ... motor deg", the average of both lift encoders.
Travel limits check each encoder individually, not just the average.
"Lift: encoder error" means a lift encoder reading is unavailable.

CURRENT SETUP NOTES
-------------------
Lift up / normal return : 100 RPM, red cartridges.
Slow scoring lower     : D-pad Down, 60 RPM.
Manual down            : L2, 40 RPM.
Intake                 : port 2, green cartridge setting; R1 in, R2 out.
Claw                   : ADI H; Y toggles on press.
Claw tilt              : ADI G; always extended, no controls.
Lift motors            : ports 1 (reversed) and 10, red cartridges.
Lift reset             : D-pad Right; separate from the intake controls.
IMU                    : smart port 14; move the IMU cable here.
Horizontal rotation    : smart port 7.

Tuning and button assignments are in include/robot/config.hpp:
  liftUpperLimitDegrees       : upper limit (currently 300).
  liftClawPickupDegrees       : closed-claw clearance (currently 10).
  liftClawReturnMaxDegrees    : near-floor return band, automatically 2x clearance.
  liftDownSlowVelocityRpm     : held-D-pad-Down speed (currently 60).
  liftManualDownVelocityRpm   : held-L2 speed (currently 40).
  liftUpVelocityRpm           : up and normal return speed (currently 100).
  liftClawOpenWaitMs          : minimum scoring delay (currently 250).
  liftPullAwayDistanceIn      : required drive-away distance (currently 3).

AUTONOMOUS - FIRST-PASS SIMPLE ROUTINE
------------------------------------
ramsete_auton_example() is currently selected in src/main.cpp. To select
simple_auton(), uncomment that call there. The simple auton closes the preload
claw and tests the lift up and back down. The lift helper clamps its requested
200 motor degrees to the configured upper limit and claw-dependent floor;
the zero target resets to encoder zero.

The encoder drive helpers preserve encoder zero and units, so odometry and
driver scoring pull-away detection can continue after this routine. Distances
and turns are still measured using drive motor encoders: wheel slip can cause
position error. Ramsete and DSR implementations have not been changed.

WHERE TO CHANGE PORTS AND TUNE THE ROBOT
--------------------------------------
PORTS, MOTOR DIRECTION, AND CARTRIDGES -> src/robot/hardware.cpp
Edit the existing device definitions here; do not create a second copy.

  liftMotors({-1, 10}, pros::MotorGearset::red)
    The lift uses ports 1 (reversed) and 10.
    A negative port reverses that motor.
    Set reversal so BOTH encoders increase when the lift rises.
    Keep red for the current 100 RPM cartridges; use the actual cartridge type.

  intakeMotors({2}, pros::MotorGearset::green)
    Intake motor is on port 2. Green is provisional; change it if the installed
    motor has a different cartridge.

  leftMotors({11, -12, -13}, pros::MotorGearset::blue)
  rightMotors({18, 19, -20}, pros::MotorGearset::blue)
    These are the drivetrain ports and motor directions.

  OpenClaw('H', true, false)
    The claw uses ADI port H. Startup calls retract() to close it for preload.

  ClawTilt('G', true, false)
    The tilt cylinder uses ADI port G and stays extended. Tilt control has
    been removed; reset, claw, intake, and lift commands do not change it.

  imu(14) / horizontalEnc(-7)
    The IMU is on smart port 14; move its cable to match. The horizontal
    rotation sensor stays on smart port 7.
    Each smart device needs its own port; avoid duplicate assignments.

DECLARATIONS -> include/robot/hardware.hpp
  This file exposes the existing devices to other source files.
  Changing a port does NOT require changing its extern declaration here.
  Add a matching extern only when adding a new named hardware object.

LIFT TUNING AND BUTTONS -> include/robot/config.hpp
  Use the lift settings listed in CURRENT SETUP NOTES above.
  Change liftUpButton, liftDownButton, liftManualDownButton, liftResetButton, or
  openClawToggleButton to remap the driver controls.
  Keep liftClawPickupDegrees below the upper limit and the slow lowering
  speed below the normal lift speed. Angles are motor degrees; speeds are RPM.

MECHANISM BEHAVIOR
  src/robot/lift.cpp : Raising, scoring, pickup clearance, and reset logic.
  src/robot/claw.cpp : Claw toggle and pickup assistance.
  src/main.cpp      : Startup, Brain display, driver loop, and auton selection.

DRIVETRAIN AND PATH TUNING
  include/robot/config.hpp : Wheel diameter/RPM, track width, Ramsete effective
                            track width and acceleration limit, DSR settings.
  src/robot/hardware.cpp  : LemLib linear/angular controllers and tracking-wheel
                           diameter/offset. Offsets are in inches.
  src/22020Vlib/drive.cpp : Simple-auton encoder feedback reads port 12 on the
                           left and port 18 on the right, both 11 W motors.
  src/22020Vlib/ramsete.cpp : Custom Ramsete follower gains. Preserve the existing
                            inch conversion when adjusting its B gain.

After any code or port change, BUILD AND UPLOAD through PROS. Saving a file
alone does not update the robot. Keep the lift lowered when restarting so
the program records the correct encoder zero.
